# The Memory Pawnshop · A 116-second paper edit

A daughter walks into a pawnshop to give up the memory of her last argument with her mother. At the start, she thinks the argument is the whole memory. The hospital door marks the limit of what she saw. Crossing that threshold changes what the daughter and the audience know.

## How the cut works

| Cut time | What the audience learns | What earns the time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00:00–00:15 | The daughter wants relief. The price is losing the sound and sight of her mother, too. | An umbrella closes, a door opens, and the pawnbroker stops writing. These actions get us to the bargain quickly. |
| 00:15–00:23 | She still wants the trade. He asks her to look once more. | Let the amber light fill the frame and cut to the spoonful of white porridge. The object carries us into memory. |
| 00:23–00:45 | The daughter remembers herself as the person who took the bowl away and slammed the door. | Keep the uneaten spoonful, the mother's question and the daughter's reply in order. Hold a beat after the slam so her present-day regret can enter. |
| 00:45–01:04 | The mother kept knitting after her daughter left. | Return to the same room at night. Follow the needles, the rust-brown scarf and the blue box into the drawer. The audience sees a gift taking shape. The words of the letter still belong to the final scene. |
| 01:04–01:16 | The daughter recognizes what she has been trying to discard about herself. | Two childhood images carry the change in her thinking. Use the tilted umbrella and the embrace under her voice-over. |
| 01:16–01:30 | She accepts the cost of keeping the memory. | Let the movement of both hands pushing the vial back make her decision visible. Give her thanks its own beat as she leaves. |
| 01:30–01:56 | Opening the gift finally lets her hear her mother's answer. | Reveal the scarf before the letter. Hold two seconds after “妈妈没有怪你”. Let her face relax before the voice resumes. End with her wearing the scarf. |

## Apply the method to another script

1. Write down what the protagonist believes in the opening and which later action shows that belief has changed. Find the moment that gives the audience new evidence.
2. Decide when each piece of evidence becomes visible or audible. Here the scarf can appear in the hospital, while the letter is heard only when the daughter opens the box. In another story, find its own object, line or action that controls the reveal.
3. Build two clocks. Give each scene a target interval on the short timeline. Keep a separate source locator for the editor to find the footage. The first two scenes in this example share one approximate source range. Where a source time is unknown, identify the action or line to search for.
4. Read the dialogue at performance speed. Budget time for reactions, object handling, silence and the last image. Move or trim a redundant beat before shortening the pause that makes a reveal land.
5. Hand off the timed script, scene durations, speaker and voice-over cues, protected pauses and source selections. At storyboard stage a scene can span several shots. Choose the shot boundaries for the new project's rhythm.

# 《记忆典当行》精剪剧本

**预计成片 1 分 56 秒｜原片约 9 分钟｜人物：女儿、母亲、典当行掌柜**

女儿想忘掉和母亲最后的争吵。她记得自己摔上了病房的门，却不知道门关上后，母亲还在为她准备生日礼物。短版把这扇门作为转折，把母亲信里的话留到女儿亲手拆开礼物时才揭晓。

以下时间码为精剪版时间。对白依据原片删节、合句，原片取材区间附在文末。

## 第一场　雨夜，典当行｜00:00–00:15（15 秒）

雨巷。女儿收伞，推开木门。切柜台，掌柜仍低着头写账。

**掌柜**　想典当什么？

**女儿**　一段记忆，跟我妈妈有关的。

掌柜停笔，抬眼看她。

**掌柜**　当了，痛苦会消失。可她的声音、她的样子，也会变淡。

## 第二场　柜台上的琥珀色小瓶｜00:15–00:23（8 秒）

**女儿**　那些画面，我一天都不想再看见了。

**掌柜**　典当之前，再看一遍。看清楚了，再决定。

小瓶里亮起金光。光铺满画面，切到病房里一勺白粥。

## 第三场　病房，门关上以前｜00:23–00:45（22 秒）

女儿吹了吹粥，递到母亲嘴边。母亲闭上眼，勺子悬在半空。

**母亲**　我吃不下。

**女儿**　吃不下也要吃。我每天下班赶过来，你就这个样子？

母亲抬手去碰碗。女儿把碗收了回来。

**母亲**　妈妈是不是……拖累你了？

**女儿**　你要是早点听我的，就不会拖到现在。

母亲转过脸。女儿起身离开。门砰地合上，留一拍。

**女儿（现在的声音）**　我摔门走了，再也没有机会说对不起。

## 第四场　病房，门关上以后｜00:45–01:04（19 秒）

记忆的光幕前，女儿垂下眼睛。

**女儿**　够了。

**掌柜（画外）**　你每次都只看到这里。门关上之后呢？

切回同一间病房，已经入夜。床头灯下，母亲手里的两根针轻轻交错。停针时，她把织好的红棕色围巾贴在胸口，又仔细叠进蓝盒。

**母亲（轻声）**　生日快乐。

母亲把系好丝带的盒子抱了一下，收进抽屉。光幕这边，女儿伸出手，指尖停在母亲的影像上。

## 第五场　记忆里还有别的日子｜01:04–01:16（12 秒）

两幅旧画面在她身边亮起：小时候，母亲把伞斜向她；另一天，母亲把哭着的她搂进怀里。女儿的目光跟过去，这次没有躲开。

**女儿（画外）**　我一直想扔掉的……是那个说了重话，又不敢回头的自己。

## 第六场　回到柜台｜01:16–01:30（14 秒）

金光收回瓶中。女儿低头看着小瓶，双手把它轻轻推回去。

**女儿**　不当了。痛苦拿走，那些好也一起没了。我舍不得。

掌柜收起瓶子。女儿走到门口，回头。

**女儿**　谢谢你，让我再看一遍。

## 第七场　家中，拆开生日礼物｜01:30–01:56（26 秒）

女儿从柜子里取出那个蓝盒。灯下，她解开丝带。手指摸到熟悉的针脚，她把围巾捂到脸上。盒底还有一封信。

**母亲（读信声）**　妈妈可能等不到你生日了。别生自己的气。那天你走以后，妈妈没有怪你。

在“没有怪你”后留两秒。女儿仍看着信，眼里含着泪，嘴角慢慢松开。

**母亲（读信声）**　天冷了就戴上它。妈妈爱你。

女儿把信按在胸口。切窗边，她已经围上了围巾，抬起脸，闭上眼。

**女儿（画外）**　从今天起，我带着她，好好活。

在她脸上再停一拍，结束。
